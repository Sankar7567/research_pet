## 2. Root-Cause Analysis of Previous Methodological Bugs

In accordance with Hard Rule 2 (*"A value of exactly 0, e.g. RMSD 0.0000 Å, or perfectly additive energies is treated as a BUG. Investigate before proceeding"*), an investigation into the pipeline codebase revealed two critical defects:

### Bug 1: The "0.0000 Å Catalytic Triad RMSD" Artifact
- **Root Cause:** In [`run_structural_validation.py`](file:///home/sankar/Desktop/research_2/run_structural_validation.py), the modeled structure was generated by loading `5XG0.pdb` and mutating sidechains at positions 1, 19, 22, 34, 74, 84, 97, and 259. The rest of the protein—including the catalytic triad residues (Ser131, Asp177, His208 in PDB numbering)—was left completely unminimized and unrelaxed.
- When the script computed `RMSD(triad_cand, triad_WT)`, it calculated the Euclidean distance between identical, untouched atomic coordinates copied directly from the template:
  $$\text{RMSD} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{12} \sum_{i=1}^{12} \|\mathbf{x}_i^{WT} - \mathbf{x}_i^{WT}\|^2} \equiv 0.0000\text{ \AA}$$
- **Verdict:** Reporting $0.0000\text{ \AA}$ as evidence of "invariant catalytic geometry" was a **profound methodological bug**. In any real physical or simulated environment, distal mutations and disulfide staples induce non-zero backbone strain.

### Bug 2: Purely Additive Linear Energy Heuristics
- **Root Cause:** In [`compute_thermodynamics.py`](file:///home/sankar/Desktop/research_2/compute_thermodynamics.py) (lines 154–176), the reported $\Delta\Delta G$ of $-15.15\text{ kcal/mol}$ was not derived from any molecular mechanics force field (e.g., AMBER, CHARMM, OPLS) or empirical scoring functions (FoldX, Rosetta).
- Instead, the script executed hardcoded linear scalar formulas:
  ```python
  delta_e_vdw = -0.15 * len(mutations)  # -0.15 * 8 = -1.20 kcal/mol
  delta_e_solv = -0.35 * hydrophobic_muts  # -0.35 * 8 = -2.80 kcal/mol
  net_delta_g_ss = -3.20 + strain  # -3.12 + -3.02 = -6.14 kcal/mol

```
- **Verdict:** The value of $-15.15\text{ kcal/mol}$ was an **unphysical, perfectly additive heuristic calculation**. It completely neglected repulsive Lennard-Jones terms, electrostatic penalties, desolvation penalties, and non-additive epistasis. Labeling this heuristic output as a "predicted melting temperature of $88.7^\circ\text{C}$" violated standard biophysical rigor.

## 7. Plain-Language Limitations Statement

1. **No Computational Result Constitutes Validation:** All reported values ($\Delta\Delta G$, $\Delta T_m$, LLR, RMSD) are computational predictions subject to force field simplifications, parameter uncertainties, and static structural assumptions. No enzyme candidate may be described as "validated" or "discovered" until its physical existence, folding, and catalytic depolymerization activity have been experimentally demonstrated in a wet-lab environment.
2. **Predictive Uncertainty:** State-of-the-art computational $\Delta\Delta G$ prediction methods have typical root-mean-square errors of $\pm 1.0\text{ to }1.5\text{ kcal/mol}$. Translating predicted free energy changes directly into melting temperature shifts without empirical calibration curves is physically unjustified.
3. **Patent Status:** Automated sequence comparisons against small collections of published papers do not establish intellectual property novelty. Formal freedom-to-operate requires comprehensive patent search and legal counsel.
